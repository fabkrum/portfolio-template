# The Lawyer's checkpoint

The end of the Lawyer's block: the legal page, `site/privacy.html`, from the Lawyer's template, with its legal notice, privacy notice and accessibility statement. When you jump here, its blanks are filled in from your content file: your name, the email address in your links, today's date, and the address, phone number and VAT number if your content file has them, plus a section about your photo if your site shows one. The page is in English. A legal page the Lawyer has already written for you stays as it is.
